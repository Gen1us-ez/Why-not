The Science of Dough

Why Our Donuts Hit Different

We spent 3 years perfecting the ratio of enriched brioche dough to small-batch glaze so that every bite yields an ultra-light, pillowy crumb without greasy residue.

🌾

24h Cold Fermentation

Slow refrigeration allows complex wild yeast flavor development and breaks down gluten into an airy, cloud-like bite.

🧈

Normandy Cultured Butter

High butterfat cultured butter gives our brioche an unmistakably rich, velvety texture and fragrant brioche aroma.

🍓

Real Farm Fruit Glazes

Zero synthetic dyes or artificial flavor packets. All colors are derived purely from real strawberries, ube, and matcha.

♻️

Zero-Waste Daily Bake

Surplus daily batches are donated to local community food pantries, and spent oil is recycled into renewable biofuel.
